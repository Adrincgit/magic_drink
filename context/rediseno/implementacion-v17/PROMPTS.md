## Bunny: seis poses

Referencia: `src/components/marketplace/Animalitos y victorias en casa.webp`.

Use case: illustration-story. Production asset: 6-frame animation sprite sheet for a friendly Magic Bunny welcoming visitors to WonderPop Plaza. Reference image is the user's own anime illustration: match the WHITE PINK rabbit characters, NOT the girl, NOT her clothes. Create a truly transparent RGBA background. Landscape 1536x1024, EXACT grid of 3 columns and 2 rows of equal 512x512 cells. One and the same little round bunny fully visible in EACH cell; same head, long pink inner ears, white/cream fur, large glossy dark oval eyes, tiny heart nose, rosy cheek circles, tiny round tail, short limbs. No clothes, no hat, no bow, no jewelry, no human anatomy details, this is a simple wholesome cartoon rabbit. Warm gold light from upper left, lilac cel shading lower right, clean expressive dark plum ink outlines. In every cell the feet touch the SAME baseline at y=92% of that cell, the bunny is centered, ears keep 8% top margin. Exact consistent character size and body proportions. Six consecutive subtle waving animation poses arranged left-to-right then next row: 1 relaxed with right paw raised; 2 raised paw outward and ears perk; 3 closed-eye happy smile and paw inward; 4 open eyes paw outward, one ear bends slightly; 5 little happy cheek lift and paw inward; 6 return almost to first pose. Feet firmly planted, no jumping, no moving sideways. Both feet entirely present and same baseline; small soft contact shadow close to feet. Empty transparent space between all six sprites; no grid lines, no background, no typography, no props. High quality hand drawn animation cels intended for a seamless 6-frame loop; never morph the identity between frames.
